# Emerging Trends

## Spatial Design (AR/VR)
- 3D and AR overlays are moving from experimental to practical
- Best use cases: product visualization in e-commerce, training interfaces, data exploration, collaborative workspaces
- Doesn't require a full 3D redesign — integrate spatial elements where they genuinely improve comprehension

## Custom Cursors
- Cursors are becoming interactive brand elements — morphing shapes, sparkly trails, responsive feedback
- Used well, they enhance engagement; used poorly, they distract and frustrate
- Always provide a standard fallback for accessibility

## Immersive Hero Sections
- Fragmented layouts, scroll-triggered animations, and layered depth effects are replacing static hero images
- Video backgrounds are still used but must be lightweight and provide a pause option

## Bento Grids & Card-Based UIs
- Modular, Pinterest-style card grids dominate dashboards, feature showcases, and landing pages
- Clean, scannable, and easy to scale with new content
