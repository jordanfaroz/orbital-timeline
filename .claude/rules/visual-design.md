# Visual Design

## Color
- Maintain a **4.5:1 contrast ratio** for body text (WCAG 2.2)
- Large or bold text requires a minimum **3:1** ratio
- Use color purposefully — not just decoratively; pair it with icons or labels to convey meaning
- Always design for **both light and dark modes** — dark mode is now a baseline expectation, not a bonus feature

## Dark Mode
- Dark mode is no longer a trend — it's a product requirement in 2025
- Design both modes simultaneously, not as an afterthought
- Test color palettes in both modes; many colors shift dramatically

## Depth & Texture
- **Glassmorphism** (frosted glass aesthetics with backdrop blur) continues to add depth without heavy visual weight
- **Neomorphism** (soft shadows + subtle gradients) provides tactile realism — use sparingly and ensure accessibility
- Smooth gradients (inspired by Apple macOS and Microsoft Fluent Design) remain popular for hero sections and backgrounds
- **3D elements** are entering mainstream design, especially for e-commerce, product visualization, and data dashboards

## White Space
- White space is not "wasted" space — it reduces clutter and guides attention
- Give elements room to breathe; cramped interfaces feel amateur and increase cognitive load
