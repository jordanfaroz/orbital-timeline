# Accessibility & Inclusive Design

## WCAG 2.2 Compliance (Mandatory)
- Contrast ratios: 4.5:1 (normal text), 3:1 (large/bold text)
- All interactive elements must be keyboard-navigable (Tab, Enter, Space, Arrow keys)
- Include proper `aria-label`, `role`, and landmark attributes
- All images need descriptive `alt` text

## Beyond Compliance: Inclusive Design
- Design for **neurodiversity** — clear layouts, predictable patterns, no overwhelming information density
- Consider **aging users** — larger tap targets (min 44×44px), readable font sizes, simpler flows
- Support **low bandwidth and offline-first** scenarios, especially for global audiences
- Test with **real, diverse user groups** — not just representative personas

## Practical Checklist
- Color is never the sole means of conveying information
- Focus states are clearly visible (don't remove outline without a replacement)
- Error messages explain what went wrong AND how to fix it
- All functionality accessible without a mouse
- Screen reader tested with VoiceOver / NVDA

## Inclusive Design as Competitive Advantage
- Accessible design improves experience for **all users**, not just those with disabilities
- Better performance on SEO, legal compliance (ADA, EAA), and brand reputation
