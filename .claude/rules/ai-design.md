# AI-Powered Design

## Personalization
- AI enables real-time content adaptation: predictive search, smart suggestions, dynamic dashboards
- Personalization should enhance usability — not overwhelm users with choices
- Be transparent: tell users when content is personalized and give control to adjust

## Conversational & Voice Interfaces
- Chatbots in 2025+ recognize emotional tone and offer proactive assistance
- Voice-first interfaces are becoming primary interaction patterns for accessibility and hands-free scenarios
- Design conversational flows with clear fallback states for misunderstandings

## AI Design Tooling
- AI tools now generate UI from real component libraries, not generic mockups
- Use AI to accelerate: wireframing, A/B test variations, accessibility audits, copy generation
- Designers' role shifts to **reviewing and refining** AI output rather than building from scratch
- Maintain human oversight — AI reflects training data biases
